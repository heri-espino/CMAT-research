\section*{Technical supplement: identification and robustness}
\paragraph{Comparison families.} In the historical $1/2/3/4/5/6+$ user-only grid, 15 pairwise Wald tests are Holm-adjusted within each endpoint; fine positive-only and full zero-inclusive grids have 21 and 28 tests, respectively. Holm for ordered $p$ values is $\min\{1,\max_{r\le j}(m-r+1)p_{(r)}\}$. Distinct two-term curve and seven-adjacent families cannot replace these inferential specifications.
\paragraph{Measurement.} $Z$ imputes administrative outcomes before standardisation; PASS counts BV/RT/BA as nonpassing and numeric grade 7.5 as passing. Observed-numeric-only models condition on a selected endpoint. Accumulated term visits have no directly measured duration or instructional content.
\paragraph{Selection.} Same-term visit counts and outcomes are jointly determined. Unknown mathematical preparation, motivation, visit timing, withdrawal and PPA1 credit confound mechanism interpretation. Professor--period adjustment and clustered standard errors do not identify causal effects; non-rejection is not equivalence.
\paragraph{Further diagnostics.} Fused-lasso partition selection uses a penalty and a selection rule, not an identified academic threshold. Two-component Gaussian mixtures need not be bimodal. Grouped Shapley fit allocation and context-heldout skill are not causal estimands or enrolment-time predictions.
\paragraph{Release gate.} The PPA-group run, claims-matrix resolution, original input-fingerprint reconciliation, documented institutional PPA rules, final ethics/author statements, disclosure review, PDF byte validation and administrator approval remain required. The historical Paper 2.1 PDF and link must be preserved until review.
