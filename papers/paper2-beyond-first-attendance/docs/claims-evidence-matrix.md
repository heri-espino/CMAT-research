## Hierarchy, decisions and release gate

- **Primary inferential anchor:** C01–C05 and C24, with a clearly distinguished ten-pair five-category family versus 15-pair user-only evidence (adjusted Wald/Holm and benchmark); report the unadjusted baseline as context and keep exact contrast families explicit.
- **Supplementary inference/sensitivity:** C06–C10, C18, C20. Explain family definitions, conditioning, top-coding, observed vs imputed outcomes and modeling assumptions.
- **Exploratory model analysis:** C11–C17, C19. These test distributional adequacy, penalized parsimony, fit allocation or retrospective prediction—not the historical frequency null.
- **Descriptive:** component/outcome composition and raw grade distributions (C09, C21).
- **Unresolved / excluded:** no latent classes, policy discontinuity, causal attendance effects, equivalence, causal Shapley importance, semester-start prediction, or unexecuted PPA/modality claims (C22–C23). Exclude invalid logit inferential flags (C25); align new PPA code/deck with current single-three-group charter (C26).

**Second-pass aggregate arithmetic:** Independently recomputed all 148 source-published 10/15/21/28 family Holm p-values (four family sizes × two outcomes, 0 mismatches) and checked 95% CI = estimate ± 1.96 SE to maximum absolute error 0.00000691, due to the source's more precise normal quantile. This verifies published arithmetic only, not generation of cluster-robust covariance.
