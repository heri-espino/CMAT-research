## Specific independently verified numerical assertions

1. `10_benchmark_0_vs_1plus.csv`: any-attendance minus no-attendance Z = **+0.3611839991 SD** (SE 0.0303403958; nominal 95% CI 0.301717916–0.420650082); PASS = **+0.1529165713 probability units**, i.e. **+15.29165713 percentage points** (SE 0.0145617806; nominal 95% CI 12.4376006–18.1457137 pp). Not causal effects.
2. Main complete frequency grid `0/1/2/3/4/5/6/7+`: in `26a`, seven zero-vs-positive Z contrasts survive the 28-test Holm family, but **no positive-vs-positive Z contrast** does. In `26k`, seven zero-vs-positive PASS contrasts plus **1 vs 7+** survive the 28-test PASS family: 7+ minus 1 = **+0.1448650605** (14.48650605 pp), Holm p approximately 0.0205566. That result is **not** the user-only grid result.
3. Primary positive-user `1/2/3/4/5/6+` 15-test family and user-only `1/2/3/4/5/6/7+` 21-test family have no Holm-significant pairwise contrasts for either outcome. This **does not** establish equivalence, diminishing returns or a flat response function.
4. Piecewise exploratory aggregate `followup_questions/two_part_attendance_z_pass.csv` reports `ANY_VISIT` Z +0.3065625079 and additional-visit slope +0.0360902272; PASS +0.1192345414 and additional-visit slope +0.0222548320. Those two-per-outcome Holm families, which were verified at source level, must not be substituted for exact-category families.
5. Observed numeric-only samples and grade distributions are **selected outcomes** (administrative cases excluded), not an alternative estimate on the full population. Fused-lasso cut frequencies are algorithmic selection frequencies, not probabilities of real change points; Shapley is fit/prediction attribution, not a causal decomposition.

**Independent arithmetic procedure:** split only published aggregate CSV rows, sort each family's raw p-values, recompute `q_(i)=max_{j<=i} min(1,(m-j+1) p_(j))` in ascending-p order, and compare row-wise to committed q; additionally compare each linear contrast's bounds to `estimate ± 1.96 × cluster_SE`. All 148 valid linear-model rows passed those checks. This arithmetic does not authenticate source-data extraction, model specification or cluster-robust covariance construction.
