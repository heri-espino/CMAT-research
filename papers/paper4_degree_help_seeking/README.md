# Paper 4 — disciplinary heterogeneity in mathematics help-seeking

**Working title:** *Who Keeps Seeking Mathematics Help? Disciplinary Heterogeneity in University Mathematics Support Use*

Canonical portfolio: `../../docs/PUBLICATION_PORTFOLIO.md`.

## Research question

How do formal mathematics-support use, persistence and potentially classroom-relative performance differ across official degree programmes?

## Candidate populations

- broad observed mathematics-course attempts with CMAT coverage;
- first-MU cohort;
- linked later-Calculus risk set.

## Planned analytical objects

- degree-programme CMAT use rates;
- programme mean classroom-relative performance;
- programme differences in later support persistence;
- ecological versus individual-level relationships;
- rare-programme/sample-size handling;
- degree-code changes and administrative revalidation audit where relevant.

## Contribution

Treat mathematics support as an institutional resource whose use may differ systematically across disciplinary contexts, rather than assuming one homogeneous student population.

## Journal strategy

1. *International Journal of Mathematical Education in Science and Technology* (IJMEST) — primary target.
2. *Journal of Further and Higher Education* — broader higher-education alternative.
3. *Higher Education Research & Development* (HERD) — ambitious target only if the paper makes a broader international higher-education contribution beyond a single-institution catalogue.

## Interpretation rules

- official academic degree `CLAVECARRERA` is the primary programme variable;
- programme-level scatterplots are ecological;
- small programmes remain in the data even when excluded from programme-level inference;
- do not infer individual help-seeking mechanisms from programme averages.

## Status

Potential later paper. Needs stronger programme-level theory and inferential design before manuscript drafting.
