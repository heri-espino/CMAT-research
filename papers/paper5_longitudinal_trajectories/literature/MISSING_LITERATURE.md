# Paper 5 — missing / targeted literature

## High-priority future gaps

- Longitudinal/sequence analyses of academic help-seeking across multiple semesters or courses.
- Learning-analytics studies using service-use or support-seeking traces rather than only LMS clicks.
- Sequence-analysis, Markov/state-transition, latent-class or trajectory-clustering applications in higher education.
- Re-entry/discontinuation models for student support use.
- Longitudinal mathematics-support studies spanning more than one course transition.
- STEM progression studies linking support use with course trajectories.

## Data-method alignment gaps

Before searching narrowly for a method, establish:

- the observation unit and time scale;
- whether every student has comparable CMAT coverage windows;
- how course attempts/revalidations are handled;
- how changing official degree programmes are represented;
- how censoring/graduation/dropout is treated;
- whether visit counts, binary use, or course-level states are the trajectory input.

Only after those decisions should the literature search choose among sequence analysis, recurrent-event models, multistate models, clustering, or other longitudinal methods.

## Target-journal search

Potential routes:

- *Journal of Learning Analytics* for a genuine learning-analytics/trace-method contribution;
- TEAMAT / IJMEST for a mathematics-support trajectory contribution;
- *International Journal of STEM Education* if the final estimand concerns broader STEM progression.

## Ethics / governance gap

Add literature on ethical use of longitudinal student traces, privacy, transparency and institutional data stewardship if Paper 5 moves toward learning analytics. The public-release strategy must remain compatible with protected education records and the repository privacy policy.
