## Literature families not yet represented strongly enough

- sequence analysis in higher education;
- Markov/state-transition models of student behaviour;
- longitudinal clustering of educational engagement traces;
- learning analytics of help-seeking/service-use trajectories;
- repeated-event or recurrent-use models for academic support;
- full-degree STEM progression and support use;
- missingness, observation windows and administrative censoring in longitudinal student records.

## Journal-routing note

The literature search should follow the final design:

- if the paper remains primarily mathematics-support research, prioritize TEAMAT/IJMEST literature;
- if the paper uses sequence/state modelling as a central contribution, add core *Journal of Learning Analytics* literature and learning-analytics theory/methods;
- if the contribution broadens to STEM progression, build an explicit STEM-transition literature layer.

## Claim boundary

Trajectory classes or state sequences are descriptive/model-based summaries unless a causal design is separately justified. Do not label a trajectory as motivation, habit, need or disengagement solely from visit patterns.
