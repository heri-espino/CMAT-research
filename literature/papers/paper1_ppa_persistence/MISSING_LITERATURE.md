# Paper 1 — missing / targeted literature

Track targeted gaps here instead of mixing them into the master library.

## High-priority gaps

- Higher-education studies that directly observe behaviour **after a student incentive is removed**.
- Studies where an initial institutional incentive exposes students to an academic-support service and later use is observed without the same incentive.
- Studies in *Studies in Higher Education* on first-year support, engagement, persistence, academic help-seeking or institutional support that are close to the CMAT mechanism.
- Evidence distinguishing temporary compliance from persistent use/familiarization after an intervention.

## Medium-priority gaps

- Formal academic-support use across sequential mathematics courses.
- First-year programme incentives tied to participation rather than grades.
- Longitudinal help-seeking across course transitions.

## Search discipline

For every added source, record:

- why it is closer to the CMAT design than existing sources;
- whether it supports theory, design, interpretation or journal positioning;
- whether the setting is experimental, quasi-experimental or observational;
- whether the incentive truly disappears/changes in the follow-up period.

Do not add papers merely because they contain the words “nudge”, “engagement” or “incentive”.
