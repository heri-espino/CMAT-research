## Secondary institutional framing

- `hanushek_2008_cognitive-skills-economic-development` — use only for the broad institutional chain `provision/arrangements → incentive structures → actor behaviour`; not evidence that PPA causes persistence, habits, motivation, or academic gains.

## Peripheral analogies

- `charness_2009_incentives-exercise`

These may inform incentive persistence conceptually but should not carry the educational claim.

## Interpretation guardrails

- Never say PPA causally created CMAT use or persistence.
- Never infer habit, intrinsic motivation, or psychological state from visit records.
- Prefer `institutional participation incentive`, `formal academic help-seeking`, `behavioural persistence`, and `pattern consistent with threshold-limited use`.
- The piecewise visit-count model is descriptive, not a regression-discontinuity design.
