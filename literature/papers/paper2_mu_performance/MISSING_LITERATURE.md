# Paper 2 — missing / targeted literature

## High-priority gaps

- Recent *Teaching Mathematics and Its Applications* studies of mathematics-support use and academic performance.
- Studies that explicitly distinguish **any use vs non-use** from **dose/intensity among users**.
- Mathematics-support studies adjusting for instructor/classroom grading heterogeneity.
- Studies with strong pre-treatment mathematics proficiency measures used to address selection into support.
- Studies using classroom-relative or instructor-adjusted grade outcomes in university support-centre evaluation.

## Medium-priority gaps

- Evidence on sparse high-frequency support users and nonlinear visit-count relationships.
- Heterogeneity of support use/performance across degree programmes.
- Formal comparisons of mathematics-support users within a common first-year mathematics course rather than across heterogeneous subjects.

## Search discipline

For each candidate source, record:

- population/course;
- support exposure definition;
- outcome definition;
- whether baseline mathematics ability is measured;
- whether instructor/classroom is adjusted;
- whether the design is observational, quasi-experimental or experimental;
- whether frequency/dose is analyzed separately from any use.

Prioritize papers that help interpret CMAT’s actual estimand rather than generic tutoring studies from incomparable settings.
