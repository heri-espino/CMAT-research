# Paper 4 literature — disciplinary heterogeneity in help-seeking

Working manuscript: **Who Keeps Seeking Mathematics Help? Disciplinary Heterogeneity in University Mathematics Support Use**.

Canonical portfolio: `../../../docs/PUBLICATION_PORTFOLIO.md`.

This folder is a scientific view over the shared library, not a separate physical corpus.

## Core question

How do formal mathematics-support use and persistence differ across official degree programmes, and what institutional/disciplinary factors might plausibly explain those differences without reducing programme averages to individual motivation?

## Literature families

- academic help-seeking;
- student engagement and educational interface;
- disciplinary differences in engagement/support use;
- mathematics-support participation and non-engagement;
- equity/access to academic support;
- programme/major context in higher education;
- ecological versus individual-level inference.

The current corpus contains strong general help-seeking and mathematics-support anchors but relatively little direct literature on **degree-programme heterogeneity**, so targeted searching is still required.
