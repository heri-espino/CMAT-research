# Paper 3 literature — grading heterogeneity

Working manuscript: **When the Same Grade Does Not Mean the Same Performance: Instructor-by-Term Heterogeneity in Undergraduate Mathematics Assessment**.

Canonical portfolio: `../../../docs/PUBLICATION_PORTFOLIO.md`.

This folder is a scientific view over the shared library, not a separate physical corpus.

## Core question

How comparable are raw university mathematics grades across instructors and academic periods, and what are the consequences for assessment interpretation and downstream educational analysis?

## Literature families

- grading and assessment comparability in higher education;
- instructor effects and grading standards;
- course/classroom heterogeneity;
- measurement and standardization;
- multilevel/variance-component approaches to educational outcomes;
- mathematics-assessment context;
- consequences of raw-grade pooling for evaluation research.

The current corpus is much weaker for this paper than for Papers 1–2. `MISSING_LITERATURE.md` therefore carries substantial weight and should drive a targeted search before drafting.
