# Paper-specific literature views

These folders organize the shared research library around the five planned CMAT manuscripts. They do not contain duplicate PDFs or duplicate extracted articles.

Canonical publication plan: `../../docs/PUBLICATION_PORTFOLIO.md`.

## Views

1. `paper1_ppa_persistence/` — incentives, incentive removal, persistence of support-seeking, engagement, first-year transition and mathematics-support context.
2. `paper2_mu_performance/` — mathematics-support use, academic performance, help-seeking, selection, classroom heterogeneity and observational robustness.
3. `paper3_grading_heterogeneity/` — assessment comparability, grading distributions, instructor/term heterogeneity, measurement and standardization.
4. `paper4_degree_help_seeking/` — disciplinary/degree-programme heterogeneity in support use, engagement and persistence.
5. `paper5_longitudinal_trajectories/` — full-degree trajectories, repeated support use, educational transitions, sequence/state modelling and learning analytics.

A source may appear in several indices when it legitimately serves several manuscripts. Physical source material remains under `../library/`.

## Required files per paper view

- `README.md` — scope and research question;
- `INDEX.md` — curated sources with role/priority and claim-boundary notes;
- `MISSING_LITERATURE.md` — targeted gaps for future searching.

Paper-specific `.bib` files belong with manuscript source under root `papers/`, not here.
