# General literature index

This index groups sources by cross-project scientific function. Paths point to the canonical source layer under `../library/source_material/`.

## 1. Mathematics and statistics support

### Core reviews

- `mullen_2024_mathematics-statistics-support-review` — **core**. Broad review of mathematics/statistics support evidence. Use for field positioning and recurring limitations in support-centre evaluation.
- `lawson_2019_mathematics-support-literature-review` — **core**. Review of mathematics support literature; useful for institutional context and outcomes studied in prior work.

### Representative support-centre studies

- `matthews_2013_evaluation-mathematics-support-centres` — evaluation framework for mathematics support centres.
- `pell_2008_mathematics-support-for-all` — participation and performance in mathematics support.
- `berry_2015_mathematics-learning-support-at-risk-students` — attendance/use and performance among at-risk students.
- `jacob_2018_mathematics-support-impact-irish-university` — institutional mathematics-support impact evidence.
- `mac-an-bhaird_2009_mathematics-support-centre-grades` — mathematics-support-centre use and grades.

## 2. Academic help-seeking

- `fong_2023_academic-help-seeking-achievement` — **core** meta-analytic anchor for help-seeking and achievement.
- `karabenick_1991_academic-help-seeking-learning-strategies` — classic college help-seeking and learning-strategy evidence.
- `karabenick_2001_help-large-college-classes` — who seeks help, why and from whom.
- `karabenick_2011_self-regulated-help-seeking` — self-regulated help-seeking framework.

## 3. Student engagement and first-year transition

- `kahu_2013_student-engagement-framework` — **core** conceptual engagement framework.
- `kahu_2018_student-engagement-educational-interface` — **core** educational-interface extension.
- `bowden_2021_four-pillars-tertiary-engagement-success` — engagement/success framework.
- `van-herpen_2020_head-start-higher-education` — first-year transition/intervention context.
- `wilcox_2005_social-support-first-year-experience` — first-year social support and university experience.
- `felby_2026_relationships-first-year-student-access` — relationships, access to disciplinary content and first-year experience.

## 4. Incentives and behavioural mechanisms

- `gneezy_2011_when-incentives-dont-work` — **core** incentives, crowd-out and what can happen after incentives are removed.
- `angrist_2009_incentives-services-college-achievement` — randomized incentives/services evidence in college.
- `agnew_2021_removing-incentives-online-formative-assessments` — direct evidence on removing educational incentives.
- `leuven_2010_effect-financial-rewards-student-achievement` — financial rewards and student achievement.
- `oreopoulos_2019_unresponsiveness-college-students-nudging` — behavioural interventions and responsiveness among college students.
- `blondeel_2023_nudging-procrastination-attendance-preparation` — attendance/preparation intervention.
- `koch_2015_behavioral-economics-education` and `lavecchia_2014_behavioral-economics-education` — broader behavioural-economics-in-education framing.

## 5. Observational inference, selection and confounding

- `austin_2011_propensity-score-confounding` — **methods**. Practical propensity-score/confounding guidance.
- `rosenbaum_1983_propensity-score-causal-effects` — **methods**. Foundational propensity-score work.

These sources support sensitivity/adjustment discussion; they do not make the CMAT observational design causal.

## 6. Institutional / human-capital context

- `hanushek_2008_cognitive-skills-economic-development` — broad cognitive-skills/human-capital framing.
- related Hanushek papers in the PDF/Markdown corpus can support macro-level context where genuinely needed.

Do not use this family as evidence for student-level CMAT persistence or tutoring effects.
