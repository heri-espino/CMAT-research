Janette Matthews has worked in the Mathematics Education Centre at Loughborough University since 2007. As Pedagogic Project Officer, she supports mathcentre and has many years of experience working on mathematics education research projects including the RA Eng funded ESUM-Engineering Students Understanding Mathematics; How we Teach and the HE STEM funded Graduates' Views on the Undergraduate Mathematics curriculum. Janette holds a cross-disciplinary PhD relating to textile design and laser engineering and is interested in relationships between textile design and practice and mathematics.

Tony Croft joined the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Loughborough University in 1996 in order to establish its Mathematics Learning Support Centre. He became founding Director of the Mathematics Education Centre there in 2002, and became the Loughborough Director of sigma in 2005. In 2008 he was awarded a National Teaching Fellowship. With Duncan Lawson, he has championed the cause of students who find themselves ill-prepared for the mathematical demands of university study, overseeing the establishment of the UK virtual mathematics support centre, mathcentre, in 2002.

Duncan Lawson began working in mathematics support at Coventry University in 1991, becoming Director of the BP Mathematics Centre in 1994. In 2005, he became the Coventry Director of sigma, a HEFCE funded Centre for Excellence in Teaching and Learning (CETL) and, in the same year he was awarded a National Teaching Fellowship. Working closely with Tony Croft, he promoted mathematics support within the HE sector during the 5 years of CETL funding and then with funding from the National HE STEM Programme until 2012. Duncan was chair of the Joint Mathematical Council of the UK from 2009-2012. In 2013, he was appointed as Pro Vice Chancellor for Formative Education at Newman University.

Dagmar Waller was appointed as Assistant Director of the Higher Education Academy's Maths, Stats and OR Network (MSOR) in October 2010 following a career in regional government. In September 2011 she also took on the role of Assistant Director of sigma ensuring the effective delivery of the final year of the National HE STEM funded programme of activity. Following the closure of the MSOR Network, in December 2012 she became Project Manager of the IMA led Mathematics Teacher Training Scholarship Scheme.
