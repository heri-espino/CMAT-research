## Interpretation rules that should not be weakened during editing

- Do not call CMAT attendance a direct measure of engagement.
- Do not call CMAT non-use disengagement.
- Do not call historical instructor outcomes professor difficulty, quality, or easiness without qualification.
- Do not write that students deliberately chose an easier professor; the individual feasible choice set is not observed.
- Do not interpret same-period MU state and CMAT attendance causally.
- Do not interpret post-failure switching or CMAT use as treatments whose effects are identified by later pass rates.
- Keep the programme-by-state interaction as secondary heterogeneity evidence; its incremental explanatory magnitude is modest.
