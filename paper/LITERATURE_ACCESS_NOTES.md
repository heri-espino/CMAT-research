## Gokhool & Lawson (2026)

**Citation:** Farhana Gokhool and Duncan Lawson, “Demographic predictors of engagement with mathematics and statistics support”, *Teaching Mathematics and its Applications*, 45(3), 434–453, DOI 10.1093/teamat/hrag003.

**Access status:** abstract supplied by the project owner on 2026-09-20; full article not available to the project at the time of the current draft.

### Claims supported by the available abstract

The manuscript may state that the article:

- studies engagement with mathematics and statistics drop-in support at Coventry University;
- analyses students from 12 disciplines with mathematics and/or statistics content;
- examines demographic predictors including gender, entry requirements, course stage, ethnicity, age, nationality and disability;
- distinguishes two dimensions of engagement: whether a student engaged at least once and, conditional on engagement, number of visits;
- uses a two-stage hurdle model, with binomial logistic regression for engagement and a negative-binomial model for visit frequency among students who engage.

### Claims not currently supported

Do **not** state from this source:

- which demographic variables were statistically significant;
- effect sizes, coefficients or predicted probabilities;
- model diagnostics or robustness results;
- substantive conclusions beyond the abstract;
- limitations or recommendations not explicitly contained in the supplied abstract.

The current Paper 2.1 draft cites this paper only for the conceptual/methodological distinction between initial engagement and repeated attendance.

If the full text becomes available, review it before expanding the citation.
