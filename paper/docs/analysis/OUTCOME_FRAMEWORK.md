# Paper 2.1 — two academic outcomes

**Research question:** how do final MU outcomes differ between recorded CMAT attendance-frequency groups after adjustment and Holm correction?

## Z: within instructor-period standardised final performance

The primary continuous outcome uses numeric grades unchanged and, for final administrative codes BA/BV/RT, the canonical imputation of sub-passing values. The completed distribution is standardised within the instructor × academic period grading context. Coefficients and intervals are expressed in standard deviation units and are conditional on degree and grading context.

## PASS: probability of meeting the 7.5 threshold

PASS=1 for final numeric grade ≥7.5; PASS=0 for a lower numeric grade or BA/BV/RT. No numeric imputation is used. Fixed-effect linear probability models estimate **risk differences in percentage points**, with instructor-period clustered inference.

## Sensitivity and interpretive restrictions

The observed-numeric complete-case Z excludes students with adverse administrative final states and conditions on receiving a numeric grade; it is a different selected population. Zero-versus-positive use is a benchmark, whereas frequency contrasts among users constitute the principal multiple-testing question. Separate Holm families for each outcome and frequency specification.

A non-significant comparison is inconclusive and does not establish equivalence or a plateau. No outcomes are interpreted as pre/post gains or causal effects of CMAT attendance.
