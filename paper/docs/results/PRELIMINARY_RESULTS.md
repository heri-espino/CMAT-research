# Paper 2.1 — verified historical Holm outcomes

These values summarise the existing controlled-data tables under `results/paper21/tables/`; no new model has been fitted during the 2026-10-09 editorial rescoping. Source documentation and nuanced interpretation are in `notes/05_RESULTADOS_Y_PROCEDENCIA.md` and `notes/06_INTERPRETACION.md`.

## Any attendance versus zero (N=6,627; 190 instructor-period clusters)

- Continuous instructor-period Z: adjusted difference +0.361 SD, 95% CI 0.302–0.421, p<0.001.
- PASS: adjusted risk difference +15.3 percentage points, 95% CI 12.4–18.1 points, p<0.001.

## Original positive-group specification, 1/2/3/4/5/6+

N=1,234 CMAT users, 176 instructor-period groups. Omnibus p=0.317 (Z) and p=0.152 (PASS). No positive-frequency pair survived Holm among the **15 comparisons per outcome**. For illustration, the unadjusted 1-vs-6+ p-values of 0.022 and 0.011 became 0.328 (Z) and 0.171 (PASS), respectively, after Holm.

## More detailed positive-only frequency analysis, 1/2/3/4/5/6/7+

Omnibus p=0.213 (Z) and p=0.102 (PASS), and no pair survived Holm among the **21 comparisons per outcome**. This split is secondary to the original support-based 6+ grouping.

## Eight-group model including zero

All seven zero-versus-positive comparisons survive Holm for both outcomes in their respective **28-comparison families**. In PASS, `7+` minus one visit = +14.5 percentage points (95% CI 5.9–23.1; Holm-adjusted p=0.0206). This result is from the **full-sample model**, and cannot be claimed to be significant in the user-only model. The finer 7+ tail was reported after the outcome-blind 6+ grouping had been selected.

## Instructor-clustering sensitivity

The any-use benchmark is little changed (+0.361 SD; +15.3 pp). Among users, omnibus p=0.0477 for Z and p=0.0551 for PASS. No continuous pair survives Holm; PASS 6+ minus 1 = +12.0 pp (95% CI 4.3–19.8; adjusted p=0.0358). No adjacent PASS comparison survived. This is nonadjacent, specification-dependent evidence, not a stair-step response.

## Numeric observed-grade sensitivity

Within positive attendance frequencies, omnibus p=0.397 for complete-case Z. This subset does not include administrative outcomes and cannot fully settle selection or the effects of the imputation assumption.

## Summary with limits

The strongest observational result separates non-use from any use. Within users, the main support-based Holm families yield no statistically resolved pairwise frequency differences, while exploratory full-sample or alternative clustering specifications offer some broader low-versus-high PASS contrasts. Neither non-significance nor within-classroom adjustment proves equivalence or a causal effect.
