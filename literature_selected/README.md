# Paper 2.1 literature — visit frequency and academic outcomes

Paper-specific literature workspace for `paper2.1_visit_frequency`.

The canonical branch scope, research question, outcome definitions and status live in `../PAPER_BRANCH.md` and `../paper/`. This directory contains the literature-specific layer: evidence priorities, short reading summaries, targeted gaps, selection/identification context and claim boundaries.

The physical source corpus remains shared in `../literature/library/`; do not duplicate PDFs here.

## Literature priorities

- mathematics/statistics support evaluation and academic performance;
- who uses mathematics support and selection into attendance;
- initial engagement versus frequency among users;
- any-use versus frequency/dose comparisons;
- observational versus encouragement/experimental designs;
- classroom/instructor adjustment and outcome comparability;
- interpretation of attendance as service use rather than a direct measure of motivation.

## Literature comparison now included

The 2026-10-09 audit compared full-text PDFs, the existing reading summaries, and selected publisher records, then expanded the Holm-only English introduction and BibTeX references. It added a US null-result tutoring pilot and a ten-centre TEAMAT study rather than presenting only favourable findings. The evidence matrix is selective, not an exhaustive review of all published mathematics support research.

## How to read this folder

1. `COMPARATIVE_CENTRES.md` — source-audited matrix of similar support centres, use-frequency definitions, grades/PASS, methods, results, limitations, and verification level.
2. `READING_GUIDE.md` — short Paper 1-style summaries of every entry currently in `paper/references.bib`, including manuscript role, key result and claim boundary.
3. `INDEX.md` — broader source map inherited from the Paper 2 literature workspace; it includes relevant sources beyond the current Paper 2.1 bibliography.
4. `MISSING_LITERATURE.md` — targeted acquisition gaps.
5. `ACCESS_NOTES.md` — source-access restrictions that affect claims, especially the abstract-only access currently available for Gokhool & Lawson (2026).

## Interpretation boundary

Paper 2.1 is observational. Literature with stronger causal identification is used as a comparator for design and interpretation, not as justification for causal language about CMAT attendance. Initial CMAT engagement and subsequent visit frequency are treated as distinct empirical dimensions, and visit count is not interpreted as a causal treatment dose.
