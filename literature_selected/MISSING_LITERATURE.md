# Paper 4 — missing / targeted literature

## High-priority gaps

- Higher-education studies comparing academic help-seeking or learning-support use across majors/degree programmes.
- Mathematics-support studies reporting use by discipline rather than only overall attendance.
- Evidence on disciplinary cultures, curriculum demands, or programme structures and formal support-seeking.
- Studies distinguishing programme composition effects from programme-context effects.
- Research on institutional access to support resources across disciplinary groups.

## Methodological gaps

- Multilevel approaches with students nested in degree programmes/courses.
- Methods for programme-level heterogeneity when many categories are small or unbalanced.
- Ecological-inference cautions relevant to programme-level use/performance summaries.

## Target-journal search

Prioritize literature from:

- *International Journal of Mathematical Education in Science and Technology*;
- *Journal of Further and Higher Education*;
- *Higher Education Research & Development*;
- mathematics-support journals/issues where discipline-specific participation is reported.

## Search discipline

For each source, record:

- definition of discipline/major/programme;
- whether differences are descriptive or adjusted;
- sample size per programme;
- individual-level covariates available;
- support service studied;
- whether persistence/repeated use is observed;
- whether claims are individual or ecological.

Do not add generic disciplinary-differences papers unless they have a plausible connection to academic help-seeking, support access or engagement.
