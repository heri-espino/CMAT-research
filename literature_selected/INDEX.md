## Statistical / measurement literature still needed

The substantive literature search must cover at least:

- instructor grading severity/leniency;
- between-section comparability of course grades;
- grading standards across time;
- multilevel decomposition of student/classroom/instructor variation;
- standardization and contextualized academic outcomes;
- reliability/validity of course grades as comparative measures;
- assessment fairness and comparability in higher education.

## Manuscript claim boundary

The paper should not claim that classroom-standardized `Z` is a universal measure of mathematical proficiency. It is a contextual measure of relative position within an observed classroom distribution. The literature review must keep the distinction between **assessment comparability**, **student learning**, and **latent mathematical proficiency** explicit.
