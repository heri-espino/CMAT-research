# Journal data-sharing check — 2026-09-07

This note documents the policy check used for the technical report. It is not legal advice and journal instructions must be rechecked at submission.

## Teaching Mathematics and its Applications (TEAMAT)

Current OUP author instructions state that **where ethically feasible**, the journal *strongly encourages* authors to make available the data and software code on which the paper's conclusions rely, using the manuscript/supporting files or a public repository whenever possible.

Source: https://academic.oup.com/teamat/pages/information_for_authors

Interpretation for CMAT: the wording is not a universal requirement to upload the identifiable administrative Excel file. A defensible plan for restricted university records is to provide a Data Availability Statement, reproducible code, aggregate outputs and—if useful—a synthetic dataset, while explaining institutional/privacy restrictions on row-level data.

## Studies in Higher Education / Taylor & Francis

Taylor & Francis uses several data-sharing policy tiers, so the exact tier attached to *Studies in Higher Education* must be checked again at submission. Across the publisher's policy framework, however, human-subject privacy, ethical, legal and security restrictions are explicit exceptions to public sharing. Taylor & Francis also recommends restricted-access repositories when appropriate and summarizes the principle as data being as open as possible but as closed as necessary.

Sources:
- https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/data-sharing-policies/
- https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/frequently-asked-questions/data-sharing-faq/
- https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/data-sharing/data-sharing-ethics/

Interpretation for CMAT: there is no basis for treating public release of the raw Excel as automatically required irrespective of privacy/ownership restrictions. The manuscript should include a precise Data Availability Statement, explain that administrative microdata are held under institutional restrictions, and state what can be shared (code, protocol, aggregate outputs, synthetic data, or controlled access if UDLAP authorizes it).

## Recommended CMAT Data Availability Statement — working language

> The administrative student-level data underlying this study are not publicly available because they contain protected institutional education records and were not collected for unrestricted public redistribution. Reproducible analysis code, variable definitions, and non-disclosive aggregate outputs are available with the study materials. Requests for access to restricted microdata are subject to institutional authorization, privacy review, and applicable ethical/legal requirements.

Do not promise restricted access unless the university has actually approved a process for it.
