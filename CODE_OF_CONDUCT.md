# Code of Conduct

CMAT Research expects professional, evidence-focused collaboration. Contributors should discuss methods, code, documentation, and scientific claims on their merits; communicate respectfully; document uncertainty and disagreement; and avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory conduct, or disclosure of private information.

Because this repository may reference controlled institutional research processes, privacy and research-integrity concerns take precedence over convenience. Do not post student-level data, credentials, private correspondence, or other sensitive material in issues, pull requests, commits, or public discussions.

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate these expectations, expose sensitive information, misrepresent scientific evidence, or conflict with the repository's governance and data-protection rules. Serious conduct or privacy concerns should be raised privately with the repository owner through an appropriate non-public channel rather than through a public issue.
